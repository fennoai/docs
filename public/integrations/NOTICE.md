# Third-party integration marks

These marks identify the third-party products described in Fenno's integration guides. They do not imply affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement. Original SVG files are supplied without geometry changes. Monochrome marks inherit the surrounding text color; colored application marks retain their original artwork.

The OpenAI mark identifies the OpenAI SDK; the Google Gemini mark identifies Gemini CLI. They are parent-brand marks, not claims that these libraries have separate official logos.

Source-library license labels do not replace brand guidelines or grant trademark rights. See the accompanying license files and theSVG trademark policy. The Chatbox SVG is provided with its repository's GPL-3.0 license and its original editable SVG source below.
